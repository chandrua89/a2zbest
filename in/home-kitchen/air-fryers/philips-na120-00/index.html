Philips NA120/00: a practical 4.2L choice for everyday small-batch cooking

Updated 4 October 2026 • Spec-based editorial guide

The Philips NA120/00 is a 4.2L, 1500W digital basket-style air fryer. It sits in the part of the market that makes sense for buyers who want a compact appliance for snacks, reheating and smaller meals without paying for a very large cavity.

Our view: it is a sensible fit for one to three people and for kitchens where counter space matters. For a family of four that wants to cook full batches at once, a 6L-class model may be more convenient.
Capacity4.2L
Power1500W
ControlDigital
FormatBasket air fryer
Best fitSmall households, snacks, reheating

Why 4.2L can be enough

For a couple or small household, 4.2L is large enough for many everyday jobs while remaining easy to store. The important limitation is usable basket area rather than the litre number alone. If you spread food in a single layer, crisping usually works better than filling the basket deeply.

Where the NA120/00 fits in Philips' range

This model is best viewed as a compact mainstream option. If you want more capacity, compare the NA130/00 at 6.2L. If you want a different interface or feature set, the older HD9252/90 is another compact Philips model worth comparing.

Reasons to consider it

  • Compact 4.2L footprint
  • 1500W heating power
  • Digital controls
  • Good fit for smaller households

Reasons to skip it

  • May require multiple batches for larger families
  • No oven-style racks or rotisserie flexibility
  • Not ideal if you routinely cook wide or bulky foods

Who should buy it?

Choose the NA120/00 if you want a simple digital air fryer for frequent small portions and prefer a smaller appliance that can stay on the counter. It is especially reasonable for users who value ease of use more than a long list of advanced functions.

Who should move up in size?

If you often cook for four or more people, or you want to prepare protein and vegetables in one session, a 6L+ basket can reduce the number of batches. Larger does not always mean better, but it does give you more usable cooking surface.
