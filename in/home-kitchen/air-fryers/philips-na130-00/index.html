The Philips NA130/00 is a 6.2L, 1700W digital basket-style air fryer. Compared with smaller 4L-class models, its main advantage is simple: more room to spread food and cook larger batches.

The real benefit is usable cooking area

With air fryers, extra capacity is useful because overcrowding works against crisping. A larger basket can let you arrange ingredients in a thinner layer.

Who benefits most from 6.2L?

A family of four, frequent entertainers, or anyone who cooks chicken pieces, cutlets, vegetables or snacks in larger batches will notice the extra room.

What you give up

The larger basket means a larger appliance. Measure counter depth and cabinet clearance before buying.

Reasons to consider it

  • 6.2L basket for larger batches
  • 1700W power
  • Digital controls
  • Better fit for families than 4L-class models

Reasons to skip it

  • Larger counter footprint
  • Potentially unnecessary for one or two people
  • Still a basket design, not an oven-style cavity
