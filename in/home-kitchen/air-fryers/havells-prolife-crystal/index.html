Havells Prolife Crystal: a 5L air fryer with a useful viewing window

Updated 4 October 2026 • Spec-based editorial guide

The Havells Prolife Crystal is a 5L digital air fryer positioned between compact 4L models and larger 6L+ baskets. Its most noticeable feature is the see-through window, which is useful if you prefer checking browning without repeatedly opening the basket.

Our view: this is a practical middle-size choice for small-to-medium families that value visibility and preset convenience. If maximum capacity matters more than design, compare 6L-class alternatives.

Why the window can matter

Air frying often involves checking colour near the end of a cooking cycle. A window lets you monitor food without disturbing the hot chamber as often. It is not essential, but it can improve convenience for people who cook visually rather than relying only on preset times.

Is 5L enough for a family?

For many households of three or four, 5L is a useful compromise. It gives more room than compact models while staying smaller than extra-large baskets. As always, the shape of the basket and the type of food matter; wide pieces need surface area, not just volume.

Preset convenience

The Prolife Crystal offers multiple presets and adjustable time/temperature controls. Presets are most useful as starting points rather than strict recipes, because food thickness and quantity can change cooking time.

Reasons to consider it

  • 5L middle-ground capacity
  • See-through viewing window
  • Digital controls and presets
  • Suitable for routine family use

Reasons to skip it

  • Not as spacious as 6L+ baskets
  • Window adds another surface to clean
  • Presets may not matter if you prefer manual control

Who should buy it?

It is a strong fit for buyers who want more space than a 4L model, but do not want a very large appliance. The window also makes it appealing to users who like to see progress without pulling the basket out repeatedly.
