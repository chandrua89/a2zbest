THE STORY BEHIND THE AIR FRYER

Before the rankings, meet the engineer who changed countertop cooking.

Dutch product designer and engineer Fred van der Weij began developing the modern air-fryer concept around 2006 after finding that existing small convection ovens could not make fries crisp enough. His Rapid Air approach was licensed to Philips in 2009, and Philips introduced the Airfryer at IFA Berlin in 2010.

2006Early development and prototypes
2009Rapid Air technology licensed to Philips
2010Philips launches Airfryer at IFA Berlin

The idea was not simply “hot air.” The engineering challenge was moving heated air rapidly and evenly around food in a compact chamber so browning and crisping happened more consistently.
