AGARO Regency: a 12L oven-style air fryer for buyers who want more flexibility

Updated 4 October 2026 • Spec-based editorial guide

The AGARO Regency is a 12L, 1800W oven-style air fryer. Unlike compact basket models, it uses a larger cavity and is aimed at shoppers who want more room, multiple cooking positions and accessories such as rotisserie-style components.

Our view: consider it if you have counter space and want an air fryer that behaves more like a compact oven. Skip it if your priority is fast, simple small-batch cooking.
Capacity12L

Why oven style changes the experience

A larger cavity makes it easier to use racks, trays and accessories. That can be useful for roasting, dehydrating or preparing foods that do not sit neatly in a deep basket.

The trade-off is footprint

A 12L unit is significantly larger than a 4L basket model. Before buying, measure both width and depth on the counter and leave room for ventilation.

Reasons to consider it

  • Large 12L cavity
  • 1800W power
  • Oven-style versatility
  • Useful for larger households

Reasons to skip it

  • Needs substantial counter space
  • More parts to clean than a simple basket
  • Overkill for one or two people
