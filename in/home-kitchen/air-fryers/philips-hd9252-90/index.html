Philips HD9252/90: a compact digital air fryer for smaller households

Updated 4 October 2026 • Spec-based editorial guide

The Philips HD9252/90 is a 4.1L, 1400W basket-style digital air fryer with digital controls, preset programs and Philips’ Rapid Air cooking system. It is most relevant to couples and small families that want convenient everyday crisping and reheating without moving to a large oven-style appliance.

Our view: consider it if you value a compact digital model and usually cook for up to three people. Skip it if you routinely need large batches for four or more people.

What stands out

The 4.1L size is easier to place in a compact kitchen than a 6L+ model. The digital panel and presets suit shoppers who prefer repeatable settings instead of mechanical dials.

Where the size becomes a limitation

If you often prepare snacks for four or five people at once, a 5–6L+ basket may reduce repeated batches. Air fryers generally work better when food has room for air to circulate.

Reasons to consider it

  • Compact 4.1L format
  • Digital touch interface
  • Preset programs for convenience
  • Suitable for frequent small-batch cooking

Reasons to skip it

  • Small for large family batches
  • Basket format is less flexible than oven-style designs
  • Digital controls may be unnecessary if you prefer simple dials
