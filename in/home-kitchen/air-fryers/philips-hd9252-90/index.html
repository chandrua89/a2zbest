Philips HD9252/90: a compact digital air fryer for smaller households

Updated 4 October 2026 • Spec-based editorial guide

The Philips HD9252/90 is a 4.1L, 1400W basket-style digital air fryer. Its appeal is straightforward: a compact footprint, digital controls, preset programs and Philips’ Rapid Air cooking system. It is most relevant to couples and small families that want convenient everyday crisping and reheating without moving to a large oven-style appliance.

Our view: consider it if you value a compact digital model and usually cook for up to three people. Skip it if you routinely need large batches for four or more people.

What stands out

The 4.1L size is easier to place in a compact kitchen than a 6L+ model. The digital panel and presets suit shoppers who prefer repeatable settings instead of mechanical dials. Philips also highlights a keep-warm function and automatic shut-off, which can be useful for everyday convenience.

Where the size becomes a limitation

The same compact basket that makes this model easy to store can become restrictive when cooking for a larger family. Air fryers work best when hot air can move around the food, so filling the basket to its physical maximum is not the same as having useful cooking capacity. If you often prepare snacks for four or five people at once, a 5–6L+ basket may reduce repeated batches.

Reasons to consider it

  • Compact 4.1L format
  • Digital touch interface
  • Preset programs for convenience
  • Suitable for frequent small-batch cooking

Reasons to skip it

  • Small for large family batches
  • Basket format is less flexible than oven-style designs
  • Digital controls may be unnecessary if you prefer simple dials

Who should buy the HD9252/90?

It makes the most sense for one to three people, or for a larger household that wants a second appliance mainly for snacks, reheating and side dishes. It is also a reasonable fit for someone who wants a known mainstream brand and a simple digital workflow without extra app features.

Who should compare something else?

If you cook complete meals for four or more people, compare the larger Philips NA130/00 or another 6L-class basket. If you want racks, rotisserie or a mini-oven experience, look at 10–12L oven-style models such as the AGARO Regency.
