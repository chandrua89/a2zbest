How A2ZBest compares products

Published 4 October 2026 • Updated 5 October 2026 • Editorial methodology

A2ZBest exists to make product choices easier, not to turn every page into a list of affiliate buttons. Our comparison method starts with the buyer’s situation: what problem are they solving, what constraints matter, and which differences between products actually change the experience?

1. We start with use case

A feature is only valuable when it helps a real use case. For an air fryer, basket capacity matters differently for a couple than for a family of five. A viewing window may be useful to someone who checks browning often, while another shopper may care more about compact size. We therefore avoid declaring a single model “best for everyone”.

2. We separate facts from judgement

Specifications such as capacity, power or control type are factual. Labels such as “best for small families” are editorial interpretations. Our pages aim to make that distinction clear. We also avoid inventing test results we have not performed.

3. We explain trade-offs

Every product has compromises. Larger capacity usually means a larger footprint. More presets can improve convenience but also add complexity. Oven-style air fryers can handle more kinds of food yet take more counter space and may require more cleaning. Good buying advice should explain both sides.

4. We check multiple sources

We compare retailer listings with manufacturer information and other credible public sources where useful. If a warranty, country-of-origin detail, service term or specification cannot be verified confidently, we label it for the reader to verify on the current seller or manufacturer listing instead of guessing.

5. We avoid stale retailer data

Prices, ratings, review counts, availability and promotions change constantly. We do not present those values as permanent facts. Where a rating or review count is shown, it is a snapshot and the current retailer page remains the source of truth.

6. Affiliate relationships do not decide the conclusion

A2ZBest may earn commissions when a visitor uses an affiliate link and makes a qualifying purchase. That does not change the purchase price for the visitor. Our editorial structure is designed to remain useful even if every affiliate link is removed.

7. How we use AI-assisted research

We may use AI tools to help organise product information, compare specifications, identify differences, draft summaries and structure buying guides. AI is used as a research and writing aid, not as a substitute for verification. Product facts are checked against available retailer, manufacturer and other public sources before publication, and uncertain details are labelled for verification.

8. We update when useful

Product lines change, models are replaced and important specifications can be revised. We publish update dates and refresh guides when changes materially affect the recommendation or comparison.

Our six-part comparison checklist

  • Fit: who is the product suited to?
  • Useful features: which features matter in everyday use?
  • Customer evidence: how strong and mature is the owner/review signal?
  • Trade-offs: what do you give up?
  • Ownership: cleaning, storage, serviceability, warranty and ease of use.
  • Value: whether the feature set makes sense for the intended buyer.
A2ZBest does not claim hands-on testing unless a page explicitly says that we physically tested the product. Spec-based guides are labelled and written as research/editorial comparisons.
